Privacy policy

Libela does not collect any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it sends nothing to any server.

The camera is used only on your iPhone, to show the picture you measure. Pictures are not saved or sent anywhere, unless you share a photo yourself.

The motion sensors are used only on your iPhone, to measure angles.

Your settings and measurements stay on your iPhone.

Questions about privacy: krivdaj@gmail.com
